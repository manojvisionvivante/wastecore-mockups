Phase 1 clickable prototype: 32 brief screens plus job and facility lists based on the Waste Sector Developer Pack (v1.0 and v2.0). Version 3 · October 2026.

How to review
  • Open any screen. The left menu, top bar and buttons move between screens, like the finished product.
  • Module cards, workflow steps, filters, tabs and toggles respond to clicks.
  • All names, jobs and figures are sample data for illustration only.
